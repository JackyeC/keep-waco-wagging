# Keep Waco Wagging — Merch Design Checklist

**Use before any design is marked APPROVED.**  
One fail = do not advance to Printify or Shopify.

Reference: `docs/KWW_MERCH_VIBE_SPEC.md`

---

## Pass / fail checklist

| # | Check | Pass criteria |
|---|--------|---------------|
| 1 | **Brand feeling** | Warm, local, editorial, restrained — not loud or generic POD |
| 2 | **Waco connection** | Clear Waco or Central Texas anchor (landmark, phrase, or location line) |
| 3 | **Dog-life credibility** | Passes final test: believable from people who actually care for dogs |
| 4 | **Design family** | Fits exactly one approved family (minimal type, skyline, badge, script, breed dept, Wear the Wag) |
| 5 | **One idea only** | No skyline + slogan + breed + paw collage on one shirt |
| 6 | **Dog included** | Integrated dog present (silhouette, portrait, or scene) — paw-only does not count on hero designs |
| 7 | **Layout scale** | Modest chest size; generous breathing room; not billboard-scale |
| 8 | **Palette** | Uses approved KWW palette only (`#6E7E63`, `#F4EDE4`, `#C68C86`, `#4C463E`, `#A9C2CF`, `#B3A48E`, `#E5C9C4`) |
| 9 | **Typography** | Cormorant/Parisienne/Jost rules (or Kittl equivalents with same restraint) — no chunky novelty fonts |
| 10 | **Skyline art** | If cityscape: traced from `waco-skyline-owner-approved-2026-07-03.png` only — real ALICO, connected baseline |
| 11 | **Not mood-board art** | Art is original production work — not exported AI mood board or Kittl template unchanged |
| 12 | **Not competitor copy** | No exact competitor phrase; no traced competitor illustration |
| 13 | **Garment colors** | 4–6 curated colors max; premium lived-in blanks (CC 1717 or approved equivalent) |
| 14 | **Ink discipline** | One ink color per garment (two only if spec explicitly allows for skyline lockup) |
| 15 | **Export quality** | Transparent PNG, 300 DPI, full print-area dimensions — no mockup baked in |
| 16 | **Owner final test** | “Would a Waco dog parent believe this came from people who actually board, train, walk, and care for dogs?” → **Yes** |

---

## Quick reject triggers (automatic fail)

- [ ] Generic “dog mom nation” energy with no Waco anchor  
- [ ] Kittl trending template with only text changed  
- [ ] Paw print + bone + heart cluster as the whole design  
- [ ] 15-color rainbow variant menu  
- [ ] Raw Printify price decimals ($18.82, $20.57)  
- [ ] Fake or generic ALICO / city tower  
- [ ] No dog anywhere on the design
