KEEP WACO wagging

Community · Connection · Compassion

The community and pet-care home of Platinum Scoops. This is how Keep Waco Wagging looks, sounds, and feels — everywhere, so a Waco dog family knows us on sight.

01 — Essence

Warm, local, and built around real dogs.

We are

Family-run. Inclusive & multicultural. Multi-ability & welcoming. Hands-on and present. Proudly Waco. The good-towels, kitchen-sink kind of care.

We are not

A warehouse kennel. A faceless franchise. Cold or corporate. Loud or gimmicky. An after-hours side gig. Anything that treats a dog like a number.

04 — Typography

Three voices, one tone

Aa
Cormorant Garamond

Headlines & prices. Elegant, editorial. Caps + wide tracking for the logo line.

Aa
Parisienne

The signature accent. One or two words only — "wagging," "wag," "service." Never body text.

Aa
Jost

Body, labels, buttons & nav. Airy. Uppercase + tracking for small labels.

05 — Voice & taglines
"Together, we keep waco wagging."

Talk like a neighbor who loves dogs. Warm, plainspoken, a little playful — never salesy. Lead with the dog and the family, then the service.
