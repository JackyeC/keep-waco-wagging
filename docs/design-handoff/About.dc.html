Our story

Meet Jackye & Todd

Keep Waco Wagging is the community and pet-care home of Platinum Scoops — the family-run team behind boarding, daycare, scooping, training, and event care. The Claytons built this around dogs, full-time, because it's the work they love.

No warehouse kennels. No after-hours side gigs. Just real care, in a real Waco home, for real dog families.
