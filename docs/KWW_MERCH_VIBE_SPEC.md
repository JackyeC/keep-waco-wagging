## 1. Brand feeling

Keep Waco Wagging merch should feel like it came from people who actually board, train, walk, clean up after, and love dogs in Waco — not from a national print-on-demand template shop.

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| Warm Waco editorial | Cold, corporate, or franchise |
| Local and lived-in | Generic “dog mom nation” |
| Real pet-care credibility | Faceless drop-ship energy |
| Vintage department-badge influence | Chunky novelty fonts |
| Calm, clean, inclusive, inviting | Loud, cute-for-cute’s-sake, mass-produced |
| Premium because it is **restrained** | Premium because it is flashy |

**Core pillars:** Community · Connection · Compassion  
**Anchor line:** Cute shirts. Local impact. More tails wagging.

## 3. Approved typography

### For brand & web (and merch lockup text)

| Role | Font | Rule |
|------|------|------|
| Editorial headlines | **Cormorant Garamond** | KEEP WACO, department titles, arched phrases |
| One accent word only | **Parisienne** | “wagging” — never a full sentence in script |
| Labels, locations, nav | **Jost** | WACO, TEXAS · small caps · tracking · supporting lines |

### Kittl / print production note

Kittl production files may use Oswald, Montserrat, or Bromello **only inside print artwork files** when they match the editorial restraint above. They do not replace the brand book fonts on the website or in the logo lockup.

**Never:** chunky display fonts, cartoon type, stacked novelty faces, or full phrases in script.

## 4. Approved design families

Every product must belong to one family. No hybrid mashups.

| Family | Description | Example concepts |
|--------|-------------|------------------|
| **Minimal Waco typography** | Large phrase, one tiny icon, no breed portrait | Waco Treat Department, Backyard Zoomie Supervisor |
| **Waco skyline** | Connected cityscape, dog integrated, owner-approved skyline art | Keep Waco Wagging, Waco Dog Dad skyline |
| **Department / committee badge** | Circular or arched type, vintage editorial badge | Waco Walk Committee, Waco Dog Social Club |
| **Small script chest mark** | One lowercase script phrase + location subline | dog mama · WACO, TEXAS |
| **Breed-specific department** | Original breed portrait + Waco phrase | Tiny Dog, Full Waco Energy — Chihuahua |
| **Wear the Wag** | Lockup-led; skyline or bridge optional | Hero brand tee |

**Approved concept names (launch vocabulary):**

- Waco Dog Social Club  
- Waco Walk Committee  
- Wear the Wag  
- Waco Treat Department  
- Backyard Zoomie Supervisor  
- Waco Dog Mom / Waco Dog Dad (location-stamped, restrained)  
- Breed department phrases (Waco-local, breed-specific — not generic gift-tee copy)

## 5. Layout rules

1. **One idea per product** — phrase *or* skyline *or* badge *or* breed portrait. Not all four.  
2. **Modest chest-sized artwork** — design sits in upper chest; generous blank garment below and around.  
3. **Large areas of breathing room** — premium = empty space, not filled canvas.  
4. **Garment color carries part of the design** — choose ink for contrast; do not rely on a giant graphic.  
5. **No giant billboard graphics** — if it reads from across a parking lot, it is too big.  
6. **No collage** — never combine skyline + long slogan + breed portrait + paw cluster on one shirt.  
7. **No random decorative filler** — every element earns its place (Waco landmark, dog, department title, or single icon).  

**Every design includes a dog.** This is Keep Waco Wagging. A paw print alone does not satisfy the dog requirement on hero designs. The dog must be integrated (silhouette, portrait, or scene element) — not clip-art pasted on.

## 8. Rejection test

Reject a concept when it:

- Could belong to **any national dog-merch shop**
- Looks like a **Kittl trending template**
- Uses **chunky novelty fonts**
- Relies on **generic paws, bones, or clip art** as the main idea
- Feels **loud, cute-for-cute’s-sake, or mass-produced**
- Has **no clear Waco connection** and no real dog-life connection
- **Fails the dog rule** (no integrated dog on the design)
- Combines **multiple design families** on one product

### Final test (required)

> **“Would a Waco dog parent believe this came from people who actually board, train, walk, and care for dogs?”**

If the honest answer is no — reject or revise. Do not advance to Printify.

## 9. What this spec does not authorize

- Replacing live Shopify products without owner approval

- Swapping Shopify themes  
- Treating Kittl kit PNGs or vibe boards as finished print files  

**Owner approval is required** before any design moves to Printify production upload or Shopify publish.
